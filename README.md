## Email Marketing Platforms

- [AWeber](https://aweber.com/) - Email marketing and automation service for small businesses and creators. Includes drag-and-drop builder and autoresponders. Offers a free plan for up to 500 subscribers. ([Read more](/details/aweber.md)) `Freemium` `Free Plan` `API` `Small Business`
- [Brevo](https://brevo.com/) - CRM and marketing platform offering email, SMS, and chat. Serves small to mid-sized businesses with automation and transactional messaging. Free plan available with paid upgrades. ([Read more](/details/brevo.md)) `Freemium` `API` `SMS` `CRM`
- [Constant Contact](https://constantcontact.com/) - Email marketing platform for small businesses and nonprofits. Provides templates, list management, and event marketing tools. Paid plans with a free trial. ([Read more](/details/constant-contact.md)) `Paid` `API` `Small Business` `Event Marketing`
- [GetResponse](https://getresponse.com/) - Email marketing and automation platform with landing pages and webinars. Serves small businesses and marketers globally. Offers a free plan and paid tiers. ([Read more](/details/getresponse.md)) `Freemium` `API` `Webinars` `Landing Pages`
- [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/) - All-in-one marketing platform for email campaigns, audiences, and automation. Suitable for small businesses and enterprises. Offers a free tier and paid plans based on contacts. ([Read more](/details/mailchimp.md)) `Freemium` `API` `Small Business` `CRM`
- [MailerLite](https://mailerlite.com/) - Email marketing and automation platform with drag-and-drop editor and landing pages. Targets small businesses and creators. Includes a free plan for up to 1,000 subscribers. ([Read more](/details/mailerlite.md)) `Freemium` `Free Plan` `API` `Small Business`

## Newsletter Platforms

- [beehiiv](https://beehiiv.com/) - Newsletter platform for growth-focused creators and publishers. Includes editor, analytics, referral program, and ad network. Offers free and paid plans. ([Read more](/details/beehiiv.md)) `Freemium` `Creator` `Newsletter` `API`
- [Buttondown](https://buttondown.email/) - Minimalist newsletter platform for writers and creators. Focuses on simplicity with email automation and paid subscriptions. Offers free and paid plans. ([Read more](/details/buttondown.md)) `Freemium` `Creator` `Newsletter` `API`
- [Kit](https://kit.com/) - Creator-focused email marketing and automation platform, formerly ConvertKit. Helps creators grow audiences and sell digital products. Offers free and paid plans. ([Read more](/details/kit.md)) `Freemium` `Creator` `API` `Automation`
- [Substack](https://substack.com/) - Newsletter platform that enables writers to publish and monetize subscriptions. Free to use; Substack takes a percentage of paid subscriptions. No setup fees for publishers. ([Read more](/details/substack.md)) `Free` `Creator` `Newsletter` `Monetization`

## Open-Source & Self-Hosted

- [Ghost](https://ghost.org/) - Open-source publishing platform for newsletters and membership sites. Can be self-hosted or used via Ghost Pro hosting. Built for professional publishers and creators. ([Read more](/details/ghost.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Creator` `Membership`
- [Listmonk](https://listmonk.com/) - Self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager with a modern web interface. Supports campaigns, lists, and templates. Released under AGPL license. ([Read more](/details/listmonk.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `API` `Newsletter`
- [Mautic](https://mautic.org/) - Open-source marketing automation platform for email, campaigns, and lead management. Self-hosted with extensive integration options. Community-driven and GPL licensed. ([Read more](/details/mautic.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `API` `Enterprise`
- [phpList](https://phplist.com/) - Open-source email marketing manager for self-hosted campaigns. Handles large subscriber lists and batch processing. GPL licensed with optional plugins. ([Read more](/details/phplist.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `API` `Batch`
- [Sendy](https://sendy.co/) - Self-hosted email newsletter application using Amazon SES. One-time license fee with low sending costs. Designed for bulk email sending. ([Read more](/details/sendy.md)) `Paid` `Self-Hosted` `Amazon SES` `Bulk`
